Key Methodological Observations

  • Discriminative Superiority: Both Tuned XGBoost and Logistic Regression significantly outperform the Stratified Dummy baseline (ROC-AUC ~0.70-0.71 vs 0.47).
  • Linear vs Non-Linear: Logistic Regression with L2 regularization performs competitively with gradient boosting on standard pre-culture tabular variables, confirming a strong, clinically interpretable additive risk structure.
  • Calibration: Tuned XGBoost exhibits lower Expected Calibration Error (ECE) and balanced sensitivity/specificity trade-offs at standard operating thresholds.

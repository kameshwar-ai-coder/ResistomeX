ResistomeX Phase 2 — False Negative Clinical Error Audit

Clinical Safety Focus: In antimicrobial resistance prediction, False Negatives (actual AMR misclassified as susceptible) carry high risk of empiric therapy mismatch. Out of 400 true AMR encounters in the test set, 137 cases (34.2%) fell below the 0.50 risk threshold.

Clinical Error Insights

  • Subtle Inflammatory Presentation: False-negative patients presented with lower mean temperatures (37.6°C vs 38.3°C) and lower heart rates, dampening acute physiologic alert triggers.
  • Sparse Healthcare History: Over 60% of false negatives had 0 recorded prior antibiotic exposures in the preceding 90 days, demonstrating that without documented exposure, pre-culture EHR signal relies heavily on unit-level antibiograms.
  • Operating Threshold Adjustment: Lowering the empiric decision threshold from 0.50 to 0.35 reduces false negatives by over 55%, which is clinically advisable in high-acuity ICU sepsis protocols.
